PRISONER 15
ANDROID APPLICATION PLAN — VERSION 1.0

1. PURPOSE
A minimal, offline Android morning fitness coach for a high-stress lifestyle.
Every morning, maximum 15 minutes. Only two exercise families: push-ups and squats.
No planks, sit-ups, separate back exercises, gym equipment, accounts, ads or subscriptions.
The goal is sustainable strength, readiness and consistency, not daily exhaustion.

2. EXERCISE LIBRARY
A. Standard push-ups: both hands on the floor.
B. Uneven push-ups: one hand elevated on a low, stable, nonslip stack of books; alternate left and right elevated hand. Begin with reduced repetitions.
C. Standard bodyweight squats.
D. Bulgarian split squats: rear foot supported on a stable chair, front leg working; alternate legs and count repetitions per leg. Start shallow, with a lower support if needed.
These are variations of two exercises, not four mandatory exercises.

3. WEEKLY CYCLE
Monday: standard push-ups + standard squats; moderate.
Tuesday: uneven push-ups + Bulgarian split squats; light technique.
Wednesday: standard push-ups + standard squats; moderate.
Thursday: uneven push-ups + Bulgarian split squats; light technique.
Friday: standard push-ups + standard squats; moderate.
Saturday: uneven push-ups + Bulgarian split squats; light technique.
Sunday: easy standard push-ups + easy standard squats; recovery.
Exercise every morning, but do not train hard every day.

4. DAILY SESSION
One morning reminder: "Your 15 minutes."
Open app -> choose energy (Exhausted / Normal / Strong) -> start.
Brief dynamic warm-up, then alternating push-up and squat sets, with short rests.
Default five rounds on normal days, within a hard 15-minute limit.
Exhausted mode: two easy rounds (roughly 4–6 minutes).
Sunday: two or three easy rounds.
Never rush repetitions to satisfy the timer; shorten the workout instead.
On asymmetric days, balance left/right work and alternate the starting side between sessions.

5. INITIAL CALIBRATION AND PROGRESSION
Record comfortable, technically correct capacities separately for standard and uneven push-ups, standard squats and Bulgarian split squats per leg.
Start below maximum effort, leaving approximately 2–4 good repetitions in reserve on normal sets.
Five-round repetition targets may vary slightly by round, like the original push-up app (e.g. 16-15-15-15-20), but are generated from measured capacity, not fixed for every user.
Uneven and Bulgarian variants receive lower starting targets.
Increase targets only after at least two comfortable sessions of the same variation with satisfactory recovery; do not increase both variation difficulty and total volume at once.
If sleep, stress, soreness or technique is poor, automatically suggest a lighter session.
Pain is a stop/modify signal, not a progression target.

6. COUNTING AND SESSION EXPERIENCE
Push-ups: use the Android proximity sensor to detect approach-and-retreat of the face toward the phone, with calibration, debounce and audio/vibration confirmation. Do not require nose contact with the glass. Verify sensor availability and detection reliability on the device.
Fallback: large manual count/correction controls.
Squats: manual counting in MVP; optional camera-assisted counting in a later release.
Provide clear prompts for round number, target, rest, left/right hand elevation and working leg.
Allow pause, skip, early finish and correction of counts.
Record partial sessions honestly as completed work, not failures.

7. MOTIVATION
A clean, high-contrast workout screen with one large repetition counter and progress bar.
Brief voice or sound cues; no constant coaching chatter.
Daily completion, weekly chart, total repetitions, clean-set personal records and left/right balance.
Recovery and short sessions preserve the habit; no punitive broken streaks.
One configurable morning notification with snooze; no nagging.

8. ANDROID IMPLEMENTATION
Native Kotlin + Jetpack Compose.
Room database for workouts and set history.
DataStore for preferences and calibration.
Android notifications and suitable scheduling APIs for morning reminders.
SensorManager for proximity sensor, with device-specific calibration and fallback.
Offline-first, no login, cloud dependency, advertising or subscription.
Deterministic training-plan engine; no AI backend required.

9. MVP BUILD ORDER
Phase 1: onboarding, energy selection, weekly rotation and configurable five-round workout generator.
Phase 2: proximity-based push-up counter, manual fallback, rest timer and side-switching prompts.
Phase 3: local workout history, progress graphs, morning reminders and recovery-aware progression.
Phase 4 (optional): camera-assisted squat counting and improved accessibility/voice guidance.

10. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA
Workout can start in at most two taps after onboarding.
Every day offers only push-ups and squats, with scheduled variations.
Five rounds are available, but shorter recovery sessions are supported.
Workout never requires more than 15 minutes.
Uneven push-ups and Bulgarian split squats track sides correctly.
Push-up sensor failures never prevent manual completion.
Progress survives interruptions and works fully offline.

11. LIMITATIONS
Push-ups and squats do not directly provide a complete back-pulling stimulus or cardiovascular program. This is an intentional minimalist trade-off.
A chair and books are optional household supports for variations; standard versions work anywhere without tools.
Use a secure chair and nonslip, stable book support. Stop or modify exercises if joint pain occurs.
