# The Naked Warrior: training basis

The training method follows *The Naked Warrior* by Pavel Tsatsouline. The owner has the paper book. This file holds a summary in our own words, never the book's text or page photos.

## Status

Summary below comes from secondary sources (StrongFirst articles, book summaries), not yet from the book. Replace it with rules read from the owner's page photos, and mark each rule with its chapter.

## What is known so far (unverified against the book)

- Two exercises: the one-arm push-up and the pistol (one-legged squat). Our uneven push-ups and Bulgarian split squats are treated as steps toward them.
- "Greasing the Groove": frequent practice well below maximum (about 50%), never to failure.
- Tension development (full-body tension) and power breathing are core techniques.
- Ladders, for example 1-2-3 repeated, are used for the one-arm push-up.
- The book has progressions for both exercises ("Field-Stripping the Pistol", "Field-Stripping the One-Arm Pushup").

## How the app departs from the book (owner's choice)

- The book spreads practice through the day ("Greasing the Groove"). The owner has one 15-minute morning session instead; the app keeps the book's spirit inside it: submaximal sets, never to failure, low fatigue.
- The book does not prescribe static back exercises as far as we know (from memory, not checked against the book); the app has none either.

## Owner's rules that override anything taken from the book

- Rounds are always even (2, 4 or 6).
- One-sided exercises: equal repetitions on both sides, always in pairs.
- No static back exercises. Absolute survival minimum. A session never exceeds 15 minutes.

## In the app

1. (v0.6) One-line tension cue per set (hands crush the floor, glutes squeezed, sharp exhale on effort).
2. (v0.8) Paths toward the one-arm push-up and the pistol, six steps each, with a "next step" when the capacity reaches 10. The steps are ours, from general training sources; they have not been checked against the book.
3. (v0.8, removed in v1.1.0) Optional 1-2-3 ladders on the light days. Cut to keep the app simple; back only if the owner asks.
4. (v1.1.0) Every session is two halves: the first 2 rounds standard push-ups and squats as a warm-up, then uneven push-ups and Bulgarian split squats, both sides, equal repetitions. This is the owner's design, not the book's.
